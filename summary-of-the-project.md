We are building a Native PHP Inventory System using MVC architecture. The database schema is already normalized (Users, Products, Sales, Sale_Items). We will implement this in 6 distinct phases, starting with the Database Foundation.



In summary, the app solves three main categories of problems:

1. Operational Inefficiency & Time Waste


The Problem: The current manual method is "labour intensive" and "time wasting". Staff are forced to multitask—registering sales in a book while trying to attend to queues of impatient customers—which causes delays and poor service.





The Solution: The app automates the sales process (POS), allowing inventory to be updated in real-time without manual counting or product movement.

2. Data Inaccuracy & Errors


The Problem: Manual record-keeping is "error prone" and results in inaccurate data regarding what is actually in the store. It is difficult to calculate total items sold or update records efficiently when changes occur.






The Solution: The system provides a centralized database to track stock levels accurately, eliminating calculation errors and the need for excessive paperwork.


3. Financial Loss & Business Risk


The Problem: The store faces "internal shrinkage" (theft/loss) and oversight. There is also a risk of goods expiring unnoticed because there is no alert system.



The Solution: The software tracks every item to prevent shrinkage, alerts management when stock is low , and helps identify goods that are about to expire.
